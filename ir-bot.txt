2. Phase-based progression (Preparation → Detection → Containment → Eradication → Recovery → Lessons Learned)
3. Context retention throughout investigation
4. Evidence collection prompts
5. Timeline building
6. Report generation

**Starting point:** You could fork the IR playbooks from projects like `austinsonger/Incident-Playbook` or `aws-samples/aws-incident-response-playbooks` and convert them into conversational flows.
